Sora Misao is an experienced samurai. 

While not trained as a courtier, his experience as an Air tensai is significant, and he is just as subtle as any Crane. As Karo to the Master of Air, he holds a great deal of sway, and almost certainly has the full authority of the Rooster Clan behind his actions and decisions. 

Misao is historically a servant of his clan first and himself second. Unfortunately, beyond relatively normal levels of trade and whatnot, the diplomatic relationship of the Rooster and Octopus is so young that little is definitively known about it. Misao, on the other hand, is said to be interested in placing a non-shugenja child of his outside the clan for their schooling. 

Sora Misao is dark-haired except for sprinklings of gray at his temples. He wears a neatly trimmed beard and ceremonial robes at almost all times. He is prone to seeming bouts of inattention, his gaze being unfocused and slow to respond to questions, but he clearly remembers everything said. 

Nothing is known about what assets he brings to trade at the Court this year
