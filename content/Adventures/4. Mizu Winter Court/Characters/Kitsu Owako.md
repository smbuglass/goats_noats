Kitsu Owako is a recently announced attendee, displacing a very junior Ikoma who was to attend. They are not a diplomat, rather a sensei at the Kitsu Medium school, where they are well known as a careful and logical instructor. 

The Lion have very little contact with the Octopus, rarely sending a delegation to their winter courts. This implies that it is more of a personal decision, which suggests that the visit is due to some theological or magical issue. 

Kitsu Owako is an aged shugenja, who speaks slowly, thoughtfully, and rarely. They almost never make a decision quickly, and this often frustrates their peers and subordinates. They are a Go master, although the less respectful youths often describe their strategy as “boring you into submission”.
