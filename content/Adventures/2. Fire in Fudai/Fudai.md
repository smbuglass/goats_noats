A city in [[The Goat Clan Lands]] where a strange fire broke out.

Initially this fire was expected to be a result of the disturbed kami, but it was later found that this was actually caused by a cache of Gaijin Pepper left behind by [[The Nameless Yagi]].

The party covered this up by having [[Yagi Kagore|Kagore]] duel the Matsu that was making these accusations.
